To determine the appropriate lengths for throttle and mixture cables for your Bendix RS5A fuel injection servo on a Cozy amateur-built aircraft, you can follow these steps based on the available information and experiences shared by other builders:

1. **Routing Path**: First, you need to decide how you'll route the throttle and mixture cables. It seems you have the option to run them through the plans conduit or above it. Either option can work; however, avoid making a 180° turn past the servo. Both 45° and 90° turns are acceptable as they can reduce friction and bend stress on the cables.

2. **Cable Length Estimation**: Previous builders suggest various lengths based on their configurations. One builder reported that their throttle cable was 96 inches long, which may be a suitable starting point. However, it's best to precisely measure the required length based on your specific routing path and layout:
   - Measure the distance from the throttle quadrant to the servo.
   - For the mixture cable, also measure the distance from the mixture control to the servo.

3. **Mockup Approach**: Since you don’t have cables yet, consider creating a mockup with a flexible wire or string to visualize the route. This will help you confirm the lengths and any necessary adjustments before ordering the cables.

4. **Adjust for Angles**: When measuring, factor in any turns or bends (ideally limited to 45° or 90°) to ensure the cables can freely move without kinking. Flexibility and freedom of movement are crucial for smooth operation.

5. **Consult Manufacturer Guidelines**: If there are any guidelines specific to the Bendix RS5A servo regarding cable attachment points and actuator arms, make sure to reference those to ensure compatibility.

6. **Community Input**: Reach out to other builders or mechanics who have installed throttle and mixture cables with a similar configuration. Their insights can often provide specific part numbers or recommendations that may expedite your process.

7. **Order Extra Length**: It’s wise to order cables that are slightly longer than your measured distance, allowing for adjustments as needed. You can always trim if they are too long but cutting a cable shorter that is too short can lead to additional complications.

8. **Installation Tips**: Ensure the cables are anchored correctly to minimize any movement as the engine flexes. This can help maintain the cable's integrity and performance during operation.

By following these guidelines based on community feedback and best practices, you should be able to accurately determine and order the right lengths for your throttle and mixture cables for the Bendix RS5A servo on your Cozy aircraft.
