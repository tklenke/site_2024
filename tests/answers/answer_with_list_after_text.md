For a Cozy, start with the **aircraft plans and the instructions for your specific nose-wheel/axle and wheel assembly**. The required value can vary with the gear, axle, wheel, bolts, and nuts used—there isn’t one universal Cozy “front-wheel torque.”

Check:
- The relevant **nose-gear/wheel installation plans** and any later revisions or builder notes.
- The **wheel or axle manufacturer’s manual** (for example, Matco or Cleveland, if that’s what’s installed).
- **AC 43.13-1B**, Chapter 7, for general fastener torque guidance only when the plans or component manufacturer don’t specify a value. Account for the nut’s locking friction (“run-on torque”) where applicable.

Also distinguish the **axle fastener torque** from the **wheel-bearing adjustment**: some axle nuts set bearing preload or endplay, which should be adjusted according to the wheel manufacturer’s procedure—not just tightened to a generic bolt-torque table.
